FITEQL-STYLE DETERMINATION OF log K FOR Li AND Co ON DOLOMITE
Method: component/species tableau, Newton-Raphson multicomponent
equilibrium with an electrostatic component, WSOS/DF objective
(Westall 1982; Herbelin & Westall 1999). Model: Pokrovsky 1999.
25 C, I = 0.7 M NaCl, open system pCO2 = 10^-3.5 atm.
======================================================================

  >CaOH0 + Li+  = >CaOLi(0) + H+    log K = +1.88 +/- 1.02
  >MgOH0 + Li+  = >MgOLi(0) + H+    log K = +0.98 +/- 1.02
  >CaOH0 + Co2+ = >CaOCo+  + H+     log K = +2.31 +/- 0.10
  >MgOH0 + Co2+ = >MgOCo+  + H+     log K = +1.41 +/- 0.10

  WSOS/DF = 0.532

Notes:
  - The equilibrium at each pH is solved as one coupled system: free
    metal, three surface sites, and the surface potential (Boltzmann
    component P), by Newton-Raphson with the analytical Jacobian
    Z_jk = sum_i a_ij a_ik C_i and kappa on the P diagonal.
  - One constant per metal is fitted; the Mg-site constant is tied with
    the offset -0.9 because two pH points cannot resolve both.
  - alpha = 0.004 (Pokrovsky), high capacitance, so psi is a few mV.
  - Sorption-only model; Co mineralisation (CoCO3) is not fitted here.
