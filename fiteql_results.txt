FITEQL/MINTEQ-STYLE DETERMINATION OF log K FOR Li AND Co ON DOLOMITE
Method: component/species tableau, Newton-Raphson multicomponent
equilibrium with an electrostatic (Boltzmann) component, WSOS/DF objective
(Westall 1982; Herbelin & Westall 1999; Visual MINTEQ Eqs 4.4, 4.6, 4.11).
Chemical model: Pokrovsky 1999. Metal cations bind the CARBONATE site.
25 C, I = 0.7 M NaCl, open system pCO2 = 10^-3.5 atm.
======================================================================

  >CO3H0 + Li+  = >CO3Li(0) + H+    log K = +0.16 +/- 0.38
  >CO3H0 + Co2+ = >CO3Co+  + H+     log K = -1.89 +/- 0.05

  WSOS/DF = 0.106

Notes:
  - Per Pokrovsky 1999, metal CATIONS adsorb on the carbonate site
    (>CO3H0 + Me = >CO3Me + H+), replacing >CO3Ca+/>CO3Mg+/>CO3-; the
    >CaOH/>MgOH hydroxyl sites take anions/ligands, not metal cations.
  - One constant per metal (one site), so no Ca/Mg site tie is needed.
  - The equilibrium at each pH is solved as one coupled system by
    Newton-Raphson with the analytical Jacobian Z_jk = sum_i a_ij a_ik C_i
    and kappa on the electrostatic (P) diagonal.
  - alpha = 0.004 (Pokrovsky), high capacitance, so psi is a few mV.
  - Sorption-only model; Co mineralisation (CoCO3) is not fitted here.
  - Surface charge follows the ProtoFit definition (proton and charged
    surface species only, Eq 2.2.3): predicted 0.002 to 0.025 mmol/m2,
    matching Pokrovsky's measured 0.01 to 0.02 mmol/m2. The spreadsheet's
    ~1.7 mmol/m2 came from the total ion balance (dolomite dissolution)
    and is not a surface charge.
