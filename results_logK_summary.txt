INTRINSIC STABILITY CONSTANTS FOR Li AND Co ON DOLOMITE
Constant capacitance three-site model (Pokrovsky et al., 1999)
Single-ion data, 25 C, I ~ 0.7 M NaCl, open system (pCO2 = 10^-3.5 atm)
==========================================================================

Surface complexation reactions and fitted constants:
  >CaOH0 + Li+  = >CaOLi(0) + H+      log K_int = +1.90 +/- 1.02
  >MgOH0 + Li+  = >MgOLi(0) + H+      log K_int = +1.00 +/- 1.02
  >CaOH0 + Co2+ = >CaOCo+  + H+       log K_int = +2.42 +/- 0.18
  >MgOH0 + Co2+ = >MgOCo+  + H+       log K_int = +1.52 +/- 0.18

Background constants held fixed (Pokrovsky 1999, dolomite):
  CO3_deprot   log K = -4.80
  CO3Ca        log K = -1.80
  CO3Mg        log K = -2.00
  CaOH2_prot   log K = +11.50
  CaO_deprot   log K = -12.00
  CaHCO3       log K = -4.00
  CaCO3        log K = +16.60
  MgOH2_prot   log K = +10.60
  MgO_deprot   log K = -12.00
  MgHCO3       log K = -3.50
  MgCO3        log K = +15.40

EDL parameter alpha = 0.0040  ->  C = sqrt(I)/alpha
Site densities (mol/m2): Ca 7.0e-06, Mg 7.0e-06, CO3 1.4e-05

Fit quality: R^2 (adsorption) = 0.998
   Co pH6: measured 74.393 ppm, predicted 74.393 ppm
   Co pH2: measured 80.398 ppm, predicted 80.513 ppm
   Li pH6: measured 135.262 ppm, predicted 135.262 ppm
   Li pH2: measured 135.592 ppm, predicted 138.430 ppm

Sensitivity (mean rise in SSR for +/- 0.5 log K):
   Co-Ca: 33.96
   Li-Ca: 0.9168

Interpretation notes:
  - Co binds more strongly than Li on both sites, the expected order for a
    divalent over a monovalent ion; matches the batch recovery trend.
  - One constant per metal is fitted (Ca site); the Mg constant is tied
    with a fixed offset of -0.9 log units (Pokrovsky Ca vs Mg).
  - Sorption-only model. Co recovery in the manuscript is partly carbonate
    mineralisation (CoCO3, Eqs 7-9), folded here into the surface term.
  - The Li constant is loosely determined (small uptake, near noise).
  - PREDICTED sigma0 (Section I) is built from surface species only and
    reads nothing from the spreadsheet; MEASURED sigma0 (Eq 9, Section J)
    is a separate quantity that also carries dissolution, so its sign near
    the PZC differs. alpha = 0.004 (Pokrovsky); results shift with alpha.

    produced-water cation suite (Ba, Sr, Cd, Pb ...).
